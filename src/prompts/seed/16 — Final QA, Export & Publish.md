# **4\. Rights and source QA checklist**

Create a rights-aware checklist.

Include:

-   Are all third-party clips documented?
-   Are all stock assets sourced?
-   Are Creative Commons requirements recorded?
-   Are public domain materials verified?
-   Are screenshots necessary and contextually justified?
-   Are music and SFX licenses documented?
-   Are AI-generated assets documented?
-   Are any realistic AI people, brands, or copyrighted-looking characters present?
-   Are any high-risk assets flagged?
-   Is there a fallback replacement for risky assets?
-   Are credits included if needed?

Add a warning that this is not legal advice and uncertain cases require legal review.
