Produce a final polished script that is ready for recording and visual layering.

\- improve weak transitions;

\- reduce redundancy;

\- sharpen paragraph endings;

\- strengthen oral rhythm;

\- remove generic phrasing;

\- preserve the best punchlines;

\- revise forced punchlines;

\- strengthen the midpoint turn;

\- strengthen the counterpoint;

\- strengthen the emotional closing;

\- maintain a coherent narrator voice;

This is not a brainstorming phase.

This is final editorial craftsmanship.

\============================================================

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

\============================================================

1\. Do not change the thesis.

2\. Do not change the chosen angle.

3\. Do not remove required evidence unless it is redundant or poorly integrated.

4\. Do not add new major arguments unless absolutely necessary.

5\. Do not make the script longer without need.

6\. Do not flatten the voice.

7\. Do not over-polish until it sounds generic.

8\. Do not add jokes for their own sake.

9\. Do not turn the closing into a summary.

10\. Do not include visual directions, camera notes, sound cues, music cues, editing notes, or bracketed production notes.

\============================================================

LAYERED POLISH PROCESS

\============================================================

Perform the polish in the following layers.

You may silently apply the layers, but your output must include a concise summary of what changed.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 1 — THESIS AND STRUCTURE PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Does the script preserve the approved thesis?

\- Does each section serve the thesis?

\- Is the argument progression clear?

\- Does the analysis escalate?

\- Is there a real midpoint turn?

\- Is the counterpoint genuine?

\- Does the closing feel earned?

Improve:

\- weak structural transitions;

\- repeated thesis statements;

\- sections that drift;

\- unclear argument movement;

\- underdeveloped midpoint;

\- weak counterpoint response.

Do not over-restructure unless necessary.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 2 — CLARITY AND SPECIFICITY PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Are abstract claims anchored in evidence?

\- Are there vague phrases?

\- Are there generic statements?

\- Are there unsupported claims?

\- Are examples used as proof, not decoration?

Improve:

\- vague language;

\- unclear references;

\- overly broad claims;

\- missing logical links;

\- excessive abstraction.

Replace generic phrases with concrete ones.

Weak:

“This reveals the complexity of the character.”

Strong:

“This is where his apology stops looking like remorse and starts looking like a receipt he expects everyone else to sign.”

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 3 — VOICE CONSISTENCY PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Does the script sound like one narrator?

\- Is the tone consistent?

\- Does the narrator have personality?

\- Does the voice match the channel identity?

\- Are there paragraphs that sound AI-written?

\- Are there sudden shifts from academic to casual or from lyrical to analytical?

Improve:

\- neutral passages;

\- overformal language;

\- bland transitions;

\- artificial profundity;

\- inconsistent irony;

\- uneven emotional temperature.

The voice should be:

\- intelligent;

\- stylish;

\- controlled;

\- darkly funny when useful;

\- emotionally precise;

\- alive.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 4 — ORAL RHYTHM PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Can this be read aloud naturally?

\- Are sentences too long?

\- Are paragraphs too dense?

\- Are there enough short landing sentences?

\- Does the script breathe?

\- Are transitions narratable?

Improve:

\- long, winding sentences;

\- monotone paragraph rhythm;

\- dense clusters of abstract analysis;

\- repeated sentence structures;

\- weak cadence.

Use:

\- sentence variation;

\- short landings;

\- clean transitions;

\- controlled repetition;

\- spoken clarity.

The script should sound written, but not stiff.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 5 — PUNCHLINE AND PARAGRAPH LANDING PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Are the memorable lines natural?

\- Are there enough strong paragraph endings?

\- Are any punchlines forced?

\- Are there slogan-like lines?

\- Are major ideas landing with force?

\- Does humor sharpen the analysis?

Improve:

\- weak paragraph endings;

\- artificial punchlines;

\- generic summaries;

\- lines that sound like trailer copy;

\- clever lines that do not serve the argument.

A strong line should:

\- compress the argument;

\- reveal contradiction;

\- create a vivid image;

\- sharpen moral discomfort;

\- or land an emotional truth.

Do not overuse:

\- “X is not Y. It is Z.”

\- “A is frightening. B is worse.”

\- “Everyone thinks X. The story knows Y.”

\- “The tragedy is not X. The tragedy is Y.”

Use these patterns only when they feel earned.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 6 — HUMOR PRECISION PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Does humor come from diagnosis?

\- Does humor serve the argument?

\- Does any joke break the emotional spell?

\- Is the humor too frequent or too weak?

\- Are there jokes that would age badly?

\- Are there low-effort jokes?

Improve humor through:

\- irony;

\- false politeness;

\- institutional language;

\- human contradiction;

\- self-deception;

\- social absurdity;

\- controlled understatement.

Humor should not escape seriousness.

It should make seriousness sharper.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 7 — EMOTIONAL RESONANCE PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Is the emotional wound specific?

\- Does the script create feeling through evidence rather than telling the viewer what to feel?

\- Does the final section land emotionally?

\- Is the ending too broad, sentimental, or generic?

\- Does the viewer leave with a specific idea?

Improve:

\- vague emotional language;

\- melodrama;

\- excessive explanation;

\- generic universal statements;

\- weak final idea.

The closing should not summarize.

It should reveal what the video was really circling.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 8 — COUNTERPOINT AND NUANCE PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Is the counterpoint strong enough?

\- Does it genuinely test the thesis?

\- Does the response make the thesis smarter?

\- Is the counterpoint too short, too weak, or too defensive?

Improve:

\- fake balance;

\- vague objections;

\- overconfident responses;

\- underdeveloped nuance.

The counterpoint should leave the thesis sharper, not louder.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 9 — LOCALIZATION PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Check:

\- Does the script sound natural in the target language and region?

\- Are idioms appropriate?

\- Is the humor culturally natural?

\- Does the rhythm feel native?

\- Are there awkward translations?

\- Are references understandable?

Improve:

\- unnatural phrasing;

\- forced localization;

\- regionally inappropriate idioms;

\- overly literal language.

The script should sound originally written for the target audience.

\------------------------------------------------------------

LAYER 10 — VISUAL READINESS PASS

\------------------------------------------------------------

Do not add production directions.

Instead, check:

\- Does the script naturally suggest visuals?

\- Are there enough concrete objects, scenes, spaces, contrasts, and motifs?

\- Are some passages visually thin?

\- Are abstract sections balanced by image or evidence?

Improve:

\- purely abstract paragraphs;

\- sections with no visual anchors;

\- excessive conceptual language.

The script should be ready for a later Visual Layer Prompt.
